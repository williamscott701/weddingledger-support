Do I need an account?

No. There's no sign-up, no password and no server. Everything you enter lives in the app's private storage on your own device. That also means there's nothing to lose if you never sign in, and nothing for anyone to breach.

Does it connect to my bank?

No, deliberately. Wedding Ledger has no bank linking, no card import and no ability to move money. Every figure is one you typed in. That keeps the app's permissions to nothing and means your financial details are never anywhere near it.

What's the difference between quoted and contracted?

A quote is a number a vendor gave you. A contract is what you've actually signed for. Only vendors you mark as Booked count toward your committed total, so the forecast doesn't panic about quotes you're still weighing. When a vendor is booked, the contracted amount is used if you've entered one, otherwise the quote.

What is the "forecast total"?

For each category, it takes the larger of what you planned and what you've committed — on the reasoning that you'll spend at least what you've signed for, and if you planned more than that, you'll probably spend the plan. It's the honest prediction of where you'll land, which is usually higher than what you've committed so far and is the number worth watching.

How do I split a contract into a deposit and instalments?

Open the vendor, tap the + next to Payments, and choose Build a payment plan if the vendor has no payments yet. Pick how many payments, what percentage the deposit is, and how far apart they fall. The amounts always add back to exactly the contract total.

My widget isn't updating

Widgets refresh on their own schedule — iOS decides when, and it can lag by an hour. Opening the app forces a refresh. If a widget shows placeholder names like "Alex & Sam", it can't read your data at all: remove the widget, open the app once, then add the widget again.

Reminders aren't arriving

Check three things, in this order:

  1. Reminders come with the Full Suite — the toggle on the app's details card is disabled without it.
  2. iOS Settings → Wedding Ledger → Notifications must be allowed.
  3. A Focus mode or Scheduled Summary can hold notifications back until later.

Reminders fire at the hour you choose, the number of days before a payment is due that you set on the details card. Payments already marked paid never generate one.

Can my partner see the same budget?

Not in this version. The data lives on one device by design, and there's no sync between phones yet. What you can do today is share the PDF report or a CSV export from the share sheet — and if you buy the lifetime unlock, it's Family Shareable, so you're not paying twice.

Shared planning across two phones is the most requested thing on the list. If you want it, say so in an email; it genuinely affects what gets built next.

The Full Suite, trials and cancelling

The free version is a working budget: full category breakdown, forecast, per-guest cost and up to five vendors, for as long as you like. The Full Suite adds unlimited vendors, the payment schedule with reminders, the printable PDF report and CSV export.

The Full Suite comes three ways — monthly, yearly, or a one-time lifetime unlock for people who'd rather not subscribe to something they need once. The subscriptions renew automatically until you cancel; the lifetime unlock never renews.

To cancel a subscription: Settings app → tap your name → Subscriptions → Wedding Ledger → Cancel Subscription. There's a shortcut to that screen on the app's details card. Cancelling keeps the Full Suite until the end of the period you've paid for.

Cancelling never deletes anything. Your budget, vendors and payments all stay exactly as they are; the paid features just lock again. Export first if you want a copy.

I bought the Full Suite but it's not showing

Tap Restore purchase in the app's Settings. That asks the App Store to re-check the Apple Account signed in on the device. If you bought it on a different Apple Account, sign in to that one. Purchases are tied to the Apple Account, not to the phone.

How do I get my data out?

With the Full Suite: the details card → Export gives you the budget, vendors and payments as CSV files that open in Numbers, Excel or Google Sheets, plus a printable PDF report. Amounts export as plain numbers so spreadsheets can total them.

How do I delete everything?

Settings → Erase all data wipes every category, vendor and payment while keeping the app. Deleting the app removes it all together. Neither can be undone and we hold no copy, so export anything you want to keep first.
